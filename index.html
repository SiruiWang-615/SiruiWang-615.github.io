01 / Physiological time series

Pulse waves & cardiac function

First-author research on predicting cardiovascular function from peripheral pulse waves, including cardiac function parameters and blood-supply capability in heart failure.

Separate published studies: 412 subjects (2022) and 237 patients with heart failure (2024). Related collaborative work covers ventricular enlargement and unsupervised patient subgroup discovery.

Methods: signal processing, feature extraction, classification, regression, and model evaluation against clinical measurements.

02 / Three-dimensional field prediction

Carotid hemodynamics before & after treatment

First-author research on deep learning prediction of three-dimensional carotid flow fields before and after surgical treatments. The workflow connects vascular geometry, CFD data, and point-cloud learning.

03 / Molecular simulation & deep learning

Water transport in fuel-cell catalyst layers

Coauthored research combining molecular simulation and deep learning to investigate water distribution and diffusion in fuel-cell catalyst layers.

The study connects nanoscale structure with transport properties, extending the research beyond medical data to simulation-based materials analysis.
