01 / Physiological time series

Estimating cardiac function from a peripheral pulse

Problem: cardiovascular assessment often requires more than a readily measured pulse signal.

Result: the 2024 study predicts five parameters, including LVEF and ventricular dimensions, from pulse-wave data of 237 heart-failure patients; predictions are evaluated against clinical measurements.

My role: first author of the 2022 and 2024 studies. The 2022 study uses 412 subjects; related collaborative work extends to ventricular enlargement and patient subgroup analysis.

Methods: signal processing, feature extraction, classification, regression, and model evaluation against clinical measurements.

02 / Three-dimensional field prediction

Fast 3D flow prediction for carotid treatment studies

Problem: conventional CFD is expensive when comparing complex blood-flow fields before and after treatment.

Result: the paper reports a 7,200-fold reduction in computational cost relative to CFD, with reported prediction error below 12.5% in its evaluation setting.

My role: first author; participated across the study and drafted the manuscript. Collaborators provided and debugged the DL code and performed CFD simulations.

The figure belongs to the carotid study. A separate cardiovascular checkpoint demo below shows runnable inference and visualization on a different supplied test case.

03 / Molecular simulation & deep learning

Water transport in fuel-cell catalyst layers

Problem: catalyst-layer structure influences water transport, but molecular-scale simulation is costly.

Result: the framework maps Pt/C structure and Nafion aggregates to water density distribution and diffusion coefficient, combining molecular simulation with point-cloud learning.

My role: coauthor in a collaborative molecular-simulation and deep-learning study. Published in ACS Applied Materials & Interfaces (2023).
