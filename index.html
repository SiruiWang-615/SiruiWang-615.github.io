Sirui Wang

I received my Ph.D. in Biomedical Engineering from Chiba University in 2023 and have research experience at the Institute of Fluid Science, Tohoku University.

My research interests include physiological signal analysis, scientific machine learning, and simulation-based prediction. I work with pulse-wave time series, three-dimensional vascular data, and molecular simulation data.
